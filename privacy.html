Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 19, 2026

AIORI ("we", "us", "our") operates the AIORI mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you sign in with Apple, we receive your Apple user identifier and, on first sign-in, your name and email address (if you choose to share them). We use this information to create and maintain your AIORI account.

Profile & Gameplay Data

We store the username, display title, avatar, and gameplay activity you generate in the App — including game sessions, scores, messages you send in multiplayer matches, trivia answers, meme captions, and friend relationships. This data is associated with your account and is used to power the App's core multiplayer features.

Audio Data (Speech-to-Text)

Certain game modes offer optional voice input. If you grant microphone and speech recognition permissions, audio captured during those moments is transcribed to text using Apple's on-device or server-based speech recognition. We do not store raw audio recordings. Transcribed text may be transmitted as a message within a game session, the same as if you had typed it.

Device & Advertising Identifiers

The App displays advertising provided by Google AdMob. If you grant tracking permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, AdMob may access your device's Advertising Identifier (IDFA) to deliver personalized ads and measure ad performance. If you decline, ads will still be shown but will not be personalized using the IDFA.

Technical & Diagnostic Data

We collect limited technical information needed to operate the App reliably, including Firebase Authentication tokens, Firebase Installation identifiers used for security (App Check), and connection status for multiplayer presence.

AI Service Data

AIORI uses third-party artificial intelligence providers — including OpenAI, Google Gemini, and xAI (Grok) — to generate gameplay responses and power interactive AI features. When you use these features, the text prompts you submit, and any optional voice input you provide (after it has been transcribed to text), are securely transmitted to these providers so they can return a response. We do not send raw audio recordings to AI providers. When you create an account, we ask for your consent to this processing through an in-App notice; you must agree before you can use the App.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create, authenticate, and secure your account.
  • To operate gameplay features, including matchmaking, real-time multiplayer, messaging, and results.
  • To generate AI gameplay responses and power interactive AI features using third-party AI providers.
  • To persist your profile, progress, and preferences across sessions and devices.
  • To display advertising through Google AdMob, consistent with your App Tracking Transparency choice.
  • To detect, investigate, and prevent abuse, fraud, or violations of our Terms.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with the following categories of providers, acting on our behalf or as required to deliver the App:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Realtime Database, Storage, App Check) — backend storage and authentication.
  • Google AdMob — advertising delivery and measurement.
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple and speech recognition services.
  • OpenAI, Google Gemini, and xAI (Grok) — third-party AI providers that generate gameplay responses from the text prompts and transcribed voice input you submit.
  • Law enforcement or regulators — where required by applicable law or to protect our rights, users, or the public.

Other players may see your username, avatar, title, and in-game actions during multiplayer sessions.

4. Data Retention

We retain your account information and gameplay data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain it for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes.

5. Your Choices & Rights

  • Tracking: You can grant or revoke App Tracking Transparency consent at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Microphone / Speech: You can revoke microphone and speech recognition access in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security.
  • Account deletion: To delete your AIORI account and associated data, email the address below. We will process verified requests within 30 days.
  • Access & correction: You can view and update your username, title, and avatar from within the App's Settings screen. For other data, contact us.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA, including the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

6. Children's Privacy

AIORI is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in the relevant jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encrypted transport (HTTPS) and Firebase security rules. No system is completely secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your data and respond promptly to any suspected incident.

8. International Transfers

Your information may be processed in the United States or other countries where our service providers operate. By using the App, you consent to the transfer of your information to these locations, which may have data protection laws that differ from those in your country.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you in the App or by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

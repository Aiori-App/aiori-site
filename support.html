Support

Help & answers for the AIORI iOS app

Welcome to AIORI support. Whether you need help with your account, gameplay, or technical issues, we're here to help. Browse the topics below or reach out to us directly — we typically respond within 1–2 business days.

Contact Us

The fastest way to reach the AIORI team is by email. Please include your in-app username and a description of the issue, along with your device model and iOS version when reporting a bug.

  • General support: aiori.support@gmail.com
  • Privacy & data requests: aiori.support@gmail.com
  • Bug reports: aiori.support@gmail.com

System Requirements

  • iPhone running iOS 16.0 or later
  • An active internet connection (Wi-Fi or cellular)
  • An Apple ID for Sign in with Apple

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

AIORI uses Sign in with Apple. Tap Sign in with Apple on the welcome screen and follow the prompts. On first sign-in you can choose whether to share your name and email; either choice works for creating your account.

I can't sign in — what do I do?

Most sign-in issues are resolved by checking your internet connection and confirming you're signed into iCloud on your device (Settings → [Your Name]). If the problem continues, fully close the app, reopen it, and try again. Still stuck? Email aiori.support@gmail.com with your Apple ID email so we can investigate.

How do I change my username, title, or avatar?

Open the app and go to Settings from the main menu. From there you can update your username, display title, and avatar at any time. Changes are visible to other players the next time they see you in a match.

Why isn't matchmaking finding a game?

Matchmaking depends on player availability in the mode you've chosen. If a match isn't found quickly, try a different game mode or invite a friend directly. A weak connection can also cause matchmaking to time out — switch between Wi-Fi and cellular if you suspect a network issue.

Voice input isn't working.

Voice-enabled game modes require both microphone and speech recognition permissions. Open iOS Settings → AIORI and make sure both Microphone and Speech Recognition are turned on. If they're already enabled, try toggling them off and on again, then relaunch the app.

How do I add or remove friends?

Open the Friends tab to search for other players by username, send a friend request, accept incoming requests, or remove an existing friend. Friend changes apply immediately.

Someone is harassing me or breaking the rules. What can I do?

You can report a player from their profile or from the post-match screen. Reports are reviewed by our team, and we take action on verified violations of our community standards. For urgent or serious issues, email aiori.support@gmail.com with the player's username and details of the incident.

Can I turn off ads?

AIORI displays ads through Google AdMob to keep the core game free. You can control whether those ads are personalized through Apple's App Tracking Transparency setting at iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Declining tracking still shows ads, just without personalization.

The app is crashing or behaving strangely.

Try the following steps in order:

  • Force-close AIORI and reopen it.
  • Restart your device.
  • Make sure AIORI is updated to the latest version in the App Store.
  • Check that your iOS version is 16.0 or later.

If the issue continues, please email us with your device model, iOS version, app version, and a description of what you were doing when the problem occurred. Screenshots or screen recordings help a lot.

How do I delete my account?

To delete your AIORI account and associated data, email aiori.support@gmail.com from the address tied to your account, or use the Delete Account option in the app's Settings screen. Verified requests are processed within 30 days. Some information may be retained where required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes — see our Privacy Policy for details.

I was charged for something I didn't intend to buy.

Purchases made through the App Store are handled by Apple. Refund requests must be submitted directly to Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. If you believe the charge resulted from a bug in AIORI, email us as well so we can investigate.

Reporting a Bug

Detailed reports help us fix issues faster. When you email aiori.support@gmail.com, please include:

  • Your AIORI username
  • Device model (e.g., iPhone 15 Pro)
  • iOS version (Settings → General → About)
  • AIORI app version (shown at the bottom of the in-app Settings screen)
  • Steps to reproduce the issue
  • What you expected to happen vs. what actually happened
  • Screenshots or a screen recording, if possible
